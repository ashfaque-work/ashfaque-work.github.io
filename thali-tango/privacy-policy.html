Privacy Policy — Thali Tango

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Thali Tango ("the app") is developed by Md Ashfaque Ahmad ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information I collect

I do not ask for or collect your name, email, contacts, photos or any account details. Game progress (level, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users in the EU/UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads". Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: I never see or store your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it can be restored on a new phone. Google's handling of payment information is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
